Graphics Cycle is a professional image editing and post-production studio. We specialize in background removal, photo retouching, color correction, clipping paths, shadow effects, ghost mannequin, restoration, and vectorization for e-commerce brands, photographers, and agencies worldwide.

Simply request a quote through our website or email your sample images with instructions. We'll review your requirements, confirm pricing and turnaround time, and guide you through upload and delivery — no complicated setup required.

After confirming your order, you'll receive secure upload instructions via email or your client dashboard. Completed files are delivered in your preferred format (JPG, PNG, PSD, TIFF) through the same channel — ready to use on your store or catalog.

There is no strict limit — we handle single images and bulk batches alike. For very large projects (500+ images), contact us in advance so we can allocate dedicated production capacity and meet your deadline comfortably.

Your satisfaction is our priority. If any image doesn't meet your brief, request a free revision with clear notes. Our QA team will rework it until it matches your expectations — at no extra charge within the agreed revision policy.

Every project follows a structured workflow: brief review, skilled editor assignment, multi-step QA check, and final approval. We save your style preferences and reference samples so repeat orders stay visually consistent every time.

Our core services include background removal, photo retouching, color correction, image masking, drop & natural shadows, ghost mannequin, photo restoration, and vector conversion. We also support custom post-production workflows for Amazon, Shopify, and catalog listings.

Standard delivery is 24–48 hours depending on volume and complexity. Rush options are available for urgent e-commerce launches. We'll confirm an exact deadline before production begins so you can plan your go-live date confidently.

Pricing depends on service type, image complexity, and volume. Simple background removals start lower; advanced retouching or masking costs more. Send a few sample images and we'll provide a transparent per-image or bulk quote with no hidden fees.

Yes — new clients can request a complimentary test edit on one or two sample images. This lets you evaluate our quality and turnaround before committing to a larger batch or ongoing partnership.

We accept PayPal, bank transfer, and major credit/debit cards. For long-term clients, we also offer monthly invoicing with flexible billing cycles tailored to your production schedule.

For first-time orders, payment is typically required before delivery. Established clients may qualify for post-delivery or monthly billing. All payment terms are confirmed upfront in your order confirmation email.
